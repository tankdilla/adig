# CREATOR_STRATEGY.md

Creator Growth & Collaboration Strategy for Hello To Natural

---

# Purpose

This document outlines the strategy for building and using the **Hello To Natural Creator Network**.

The goal is to build a large, high-quality database of niche creators who align with the Hello To Natural brand and are likely to produce authentic user-generated content.

Over time this system should enable Hello To Natural to:

* build relationships with hundreds of creators
* scale authentic content creation
* increase organic brand awareness
* drive product sales through trusted voices

---

# The Creator Database Goal

The system should continuously build a database of creators.

Target database size:

| Stage   | Creators |
| ------- | -------- |
| Month 1 | 5,000    |
| Month 3 | 15,000   |
| Month 6 | 30,000   |
| Year 1  | 50,000   |

However, only a small portion of these creators will be **top partnership candidates**.

---

# The Creator Funnel

The system should narrow creators through multiple stages.

```
Discovery Pool
     ↓
Relevant Creators
     ↓
High Fit Creators
     ↓
Outreach Candidates
     ↓
Brand Partners
```

Typical ratios:

| Stage     | Percent |
| --------- | ------- |
| Discovery | 100%    |
| Relevant  | 30%     |
| High Fit  | 10%     |
| Outreach  | 3–5%    |
| Partners  | 1–2%    |

Example:

```
10,000 creators discovered
3,000 relevant
1,000 strong fits
300 outreach
100 active partners
```

---

# Ideal Creator Profile

The system is optimized for **Micro-UGC creators**.

Target range:

```
3k – 30k followers
```

These creators typically offer:

* higher engagement

* lower collaboration costs

# Sources of High Quality Creators

The system discovers creators through several methods.

---

# 1 Hashtag Discovery

Hashtags provide the initial discovery layer.

Example hashtags:

```
naturalskincare
bodybutter
melaninskincare
holistichealth
selfcare
plantbasedwellness
blackgirlwellness
pcosjourney
```

Hashtag discovery identifies creators actively posting in the niche.

---

# 2 Creator Graph Expansion

After discovering creators, the system finds additional creators by analyzing:

* tagged users
* mentions
* shared hashtags
* collaborators

This forms a **creator network graph**.

The graph helps identify entire communities of niche creators.

---

# 3 Hello To Natural Audience Seeding

One of the most powerful sources is the brand's own audience.

Sources include:

* Hello To Natural followers
* commenters on posts
* users tagging the brand
* creators using the brand hashtag

These creators are already **aware of the brand**.

---

# 4 Comment Mining

Creators who comment on niche posts often represent highly engaged communities.

Example:

A user commenting on a post about

```
body butter routines
```

may themselves be a skincare creator.

These commenters are strong candidates for outreach.

---

# 5 Similar Creator Discovery

The system analyzes creators who become successful partners and finds others with similar profiles.

Signals include:

* bio text
* hashtags
* post content
* niche keywords

---

# Creator Ranking

The system calculates a ranking score to prioritize outreach.

Example scoring formula:

```
score =
0.45 * niche_score
+ 0.25 * growth_score
+ 0.20 * partner_similarity
+ 0.10 * follower_band_score
```

This surfaces creators who are:

* strongly aligned with the brand
* growing quickly
* similar to existing partners

---

# Identifying the Top 200 Creators

Within a database of 10,000+ creators, the goal is to identify roughly:

```
200 high quality creators
```

These creators will represent the **core partnership group**.

Criteria:

* strong niche alignment
* consistent posting
* authentic engagement
* visually appealing content
* positive audience sentiment

---

# Creator Outreach Strategy

Once high quality creators are identified, outreach begins.

Outreach should be **personal and authentic**.

Example message:

```
Hi [Name],

We love your content about natural skincare and your recent post about [topic].

We run a wellness brand called Hello To Natural and think you'd be a great fit for our community.

Would you be interested in trying our products and sharing your experience?

We’d love to collaborate.
```
